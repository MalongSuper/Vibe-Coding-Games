How to play

Move the tiles

Press the arrow keys, or swipe on a touch screen. Every tile slides as far as it can in that direction.

Merge to grow

When two matching tiles run into each other, they merge into one bigger tile.

+ =

New tiles keep coming

A new tile appears after every 4 moves, and another one every 8 seconds on its own. Make room before the board fills up.

Fibonacci mode

Tiles are Fibonacci numbers. Merge neighboring numbers in the sequence: 1 + 1, 1 + 2, 2 + 3, 3 + 5, and so on. After each move, your score goes up by the highest tile that move created.

+ = + =

Winning and losing

Reach 2048 (377 in Fibonacci mode) to win. The game ends when the board is full and no tiles can merge.
